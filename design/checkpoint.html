Four steps, one signature

  1. 01

    Open a tab

    Pick a limit, a per-payment cap, an expiry and, if you want, the only services it may pay. One wallet signature moves that amount into a token account your wallet owns.

  2. 02

    Hand over a key, not a wallet

    The agent gets a tab_live_ key. It can ask TAB to pay. It can never move funds itself.

  3. 03

    The agent hits a 402

    TAB checks the tab is open, in date, under the cap, to an allowed service and inside the limit. Only then does it sign. The service is paid in about a second.

  4. 04

    Close it out

    Revoke from the dashboard or straight from your wallet. Whatever's left sweeps back to you, rent included.

limit

Hard ceiling

The allowance lives on chain. Even if TAB's own database were wrong, the token program refuses anything past it.

per_payment_cap

No single big bill

A runaway loop can't drain the tab in one call. Anything over the cap is refused before it's signed.

expires_at

Ends on its own

Tabs die on schedule. Nothing to remember, nothing left open.

allowlist

Only where you said

Lock a tab to the services you trust. Everyone else gets a polite no.
