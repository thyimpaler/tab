How it works

Four steps. One signature.

01limit · cap · expiry

Open a tab

Set a limit, a max per payment and an end date. One wallet signature funds an account you own.

02tab_live_…

Give the agent a key

The agent can ask TAB to pay. It never holds a wallet key and can't move funds itself.

03HTTP 402 → paid

It pays as it goes

Every x402 bill is checked against your rules, then signed. Anything over the line is refused.

04revoke · reclaim

Close it out

Revoke in one click. Whatever's left goes back to your wallet, rent included.
